Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about certified lab-grown diamonds, buyback terms, and bespoke ordering.

Are your diamonds authentic real diamonds?
Yes, 100%. Chemically, physically, and optically, our lab-grown diamonds are identical to earth-mined diamonds. They are pure crystallized carbon (hardness 10 on the Mohs scale) and test as real diamonds on all thermal and electrical gemological testing devices. Every stone is certified by independent bodies such as IGI or GIA.
How does The Carat Theory Lifetime Buyback policy work?
We provide full value retention security. You can trade in or upgrade your diamond jewellery at any time for 100% store credit toward a larger stone or new design, or opt for our guaranteed cash buyback formula outlined in your official Certificate of Authenticity.
How is my order shipped securely?
All orders are shipped via insured, priority armored couriers (such as FedEx Priority or Brinks). Packages arrive in completely discreet unmarked exterior cartons to prevent theft, requiring an adult signature upon delivery.
What if my ring size does not fit?
We offer one complimentary ring resize within 60 days of delivery, including prepaid insured return shipping. You can also view our international ring sizing chart or request a free plastic ring sizer in the mail.
